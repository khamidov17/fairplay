Explain why the integral test works for deciding if Σ 1/n² converges. Use a sketch or words.

The terms 1/n² are positive and get smaller, so we can compare the sum with the area under f(x) = 1/x² from 1 to infinity. Each term is a rectangle of width 1 that sits under the curve (if we start from n = 2), so the sum is less than 1 plus the integral. The integral from 1 to ∞ of 1/x² equals 1, which is finite. That means the partial sums are bounded and increasing, so the series converges.
