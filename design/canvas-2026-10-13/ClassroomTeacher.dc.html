Spec: T8 (classroom-style teacher dashboard and library)
The idea: a teacher who has used Google Classroom should feel at home in ten seconds, and find it better because exams, integrity and grading are built in. (1) Courses: one card per class with a coloured banner, the next exam, how many answers wait, and Grades and Classwork shortcuts. A strip at the top says what needs you today. (2) A class has four tabs: Stream (announce to the class, pinned notes, automatic posts when an exam is scheduled or results are released, an Upcoming box), Classwork (grouped by topic; Create adds a Quiz, Midterm, Final, Homework, Material, Question set or Topic; each row opens the right tool for its status: editor, live room, grading or results), People (teachers and TAs, students, invite by link, Canvas CSV or email) and Grades (gradebook, release, export to Canvas). (3) To review collects every class's waiting work. (4) Calendar shows the week, one colour per class, with an iCal link. (5) Library is the DKU OJ idea made reusable: Questions, Exam templates, Materials, Rubrics and one subject tab (Code starters for computer science, Formula sheets for mathematics, Diagrams for biology). Items belong to Mine, My department or University; "Use in exam" copies an item into an exam, "Copy to mine" copies it to your own, "Share" widens who sees it. What a teacher sees depends on their subject: switch the prototype control at the top. I read "library functions" in DKU OJ as shared reusable content; if it means something else (for example code helper functions), tell me and I will change the Library tabs. All names, classes and numbers are sample data.
