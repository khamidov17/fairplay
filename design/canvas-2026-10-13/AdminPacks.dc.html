Spec: A1 (superadmin)
Idea: nobody should see features their subject cannot use. A pack is a named bundle of question types plus the tools they need. Core (choice, true/false, matching, ordering, fill-in, short answer, number, essays, file upload, composite: 12 types) is always on. The other packs are switched per subject; the class's subject decides what the exam editor shows. What a switch changes: (1) the type picker for teachers hides the pack's groups and says why if a teacher searches for them; (2) the question bank filters them out; (3) the exam bundle sent to student Macs leaves out the pack's tools (code editor, Python runtime, math keyboard, audio recorder), which should make the download smaller and the app start faster, still to be measured; (4) kind defaults that mention code (homework with a practice IDE) disappear. Requests are one click for a teacher and one click for the admin. Exceptions are per teacher and per class. Every change goes to the change log. Which subjects exist, their teachers and the counts here are sample data; in real life classes come from the Canvas import. A subject with no code pack still gets everything else: live room, grading, analytics and the integrity report are identical for all subjects.
