Spec: S3 (student dashboard)
The idea: the student sees one place for everything, shaped like the Classroom they already know. (1) Home: a "Next exam" card with a get-ready checklist (app up to date, device check, charger, quiet room), three counters (due this week, missing, new grades), then one card per course. (2) A course has Stream, Classwork, My grades, People; exams sit inside the course that assigned them, so a student never sees a mixed exam. (3) To-do groups everything by This week, Later, Missing, Done. (4) Calendar shows exam windows and due dates in the course colour. (5) My grades shows only released marks. (6) Practice appears only for courses with coding work. (7) Press Start exam and the locked flow from S1 begins (start code, before lock, locked). Classmates are never listed. Sample data only; not tested in a real browser.
